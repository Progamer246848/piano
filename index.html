Profile

A talented and dedicated young pianist, Andrew Yin began his musical journey at the age of 4 in Sydney, Australia. Since then, he has quickly distinguished himself through exceptional performances and awards, marking him as an emerging talent in classical music. He currently studies under the esteemed Dr. Paul Rickard-Ford in the Sydney Conservatorium’s Rising Stars Program.

Biography

Born in the USA, Andrew Yin moved to Sydney, Australia, at the age of 1. He began formal piano education at 4 and has since advanced rapidly in his musical studies, earning accolades in prestigious competitions. In 2022, he was awarded a scholarship to the Sydney Conservatorium’s Rising Stars Program, where he continues to hone his skills under the guidance of Dr. Paul Rickard-Ford.
